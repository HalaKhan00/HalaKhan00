## About Me

I'm a researcher at the **[Bio-Optics Lab, UNIST](https://www.biooptics.org/)** (South Korea), working at the intersection of deep learning and fluorescence microscopy. My thesis focuses on building a multi-expert deep learning framework that unmixes organelle-specific fluorescence signals from blended multi-organelle images, using cross-attention-fused expert networks to model inter-organelle spatial dependencies for high-fidelity *in-silico* labelling.


---

## 🔬 Current Research

> **Multi-Expert Fluorescence Unmixing** — Developing a mixture-of-experts architecture with cross-attention fusion to disentangle overlapping organelle fluorescence signals and reconstruct individual organelle labels from blended microscopy images. Combines spatial dependency modelling with virtual staining to reduce the need for costly multi-channel acquisition.

**Key themes:** In-silico labelling · Virtual staining · Fluorescence unmixing · Mixture of Experts · Biomedical image analysis
